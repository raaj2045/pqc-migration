Fig. 8: Maximum transactions per block for secp256k1 versus ML-DSA-65 at three block-size limit configurations (4, 8, and 16 mebibytes), grouped bar chart with a logarithmic ordinate.
Deterministic computation from per-transaction wire sizes (319 bytes for a typical secp256k1 MsgSend versus 5,483 bytes for ML-DSA-65) and the block-size limit; no randomness or measurement error.
ML-DSA-65 reduces per-block capacity by 17.2x at every block-size limit measured, because per-transaction wire size dominates the small fixed block overhead.
