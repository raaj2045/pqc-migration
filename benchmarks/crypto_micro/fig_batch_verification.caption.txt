Fig. 5: Per-signature amortized verification cost as a function of batch size for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-44, on a log-log scale across batches of 10, 100, and 1000 signatures.
Single Go testing.B run per (scheme, batch size) on AMD Ryzen 5 7600X under Go 1.23.5; the reported value is the mean over the framework's auto-tuned internal iteration count and run-to-run variance is not measured. Total batch time is recorded in results.json but omitted from the figure to keep the headline visible.
At a batch size of 1000, ML-DSA-44 amortizes to 41 microseconds per signature against 145 microseconds for secp256k1, preserving the per-call verification advantage observed in Fig. 3 with no additional batching speedup for either scheme.
