Fig. 4: Aggregate signing throughput as a function of concurrent goroutine count for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-65, with a logarithmic ordinate.
Median of 10 Go testing.B runs per (key type, goroutine count), each with testing.B's own iteration count, on an AMD Ryzen 5 7600X with 6 logical CPUs under Go 1.26.5; ML-DSA-65 is measured through the Cosmos SDK v0.55 key type the chain uses, which re-parses the packed key on every call. The host exposes 6 logical CPUs, so counts above 6 share them.
At 16 goroutines secp256k1 reaches 198,768 signatures per second versus 25,950 for ML-DSA-65, a 7.7x gap (5.8x with one goroutine); secp256k1 stops scaling at 8 goroutines while ML-DSA-65 still gains slightly.
