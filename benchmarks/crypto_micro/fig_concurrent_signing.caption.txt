Fig. 4: Aggregate signing throughput as a function of concurrent goroutine count for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-44, with a logarithmic ordinate.
Single Go testing.B run per (scheme, parallelism level) on AMD Ryzen 5 7600X (6 cores, 12 threads) under Go 1.23.5; the reported value is the mean over the framework's auto-tuned internal iteration count and run-to-run variance is not measured.
At 16 goroutines secp256k1 reaches 114,377 signatures per second versus 22,061 for ML-DSA-44, a 5.2x throughput gap that closely tracks the per-call signing-cost ratio in Fig. 2 and shows both schemes scale sub-linearly above the host's six physical cores.
