Fig. 2: Signing latency as a function of message size for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-65, on a log-log scale across four message sizes spanning 100 bytes to 100 kilobytes.
Median of 10 Go testing.B runs per (key type, message size), each with testing.B's own iteration count, on an AMD Ryzen 5 7600X with 6 logical CPUs under Go 1.26.5; ML-DSA-65 is measured through the Cosmos SDK v0.55 key type the chain uses, which re-parses the packed key on every call.
ML-DSA-65 signing is 6.8 times slower than secp256k1 at 1 kilobyte (167 versus 24 microseconds), and the gap stays between 5.8x and 6.8x across the range, so the fixed cost of the lattice signature, not hashing the message, sets the difference.
