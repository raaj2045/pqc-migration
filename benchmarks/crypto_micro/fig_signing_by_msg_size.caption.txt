Fig. 2: Signing latency as a function of message size for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-44, on a log-log scale across four message sizes spanning 100 bytes to 100 kilobytes.
Single Go testing.B run per (scheme, message size) on AMD Ryzen 5 7600X under Go 1.23.5; the reported value is the mean over the framework's auto-tuned internal iteration count and run-to-run variance is not measured.
ML-DSA-44 signing is 3.6 times slower than secp256k1 at 1 kilobyte (127 versus 36 microseconds) and the gap is roughly constant across the message-size range, indicating that the lattice signing cost dominates the per-message hashing cost.
