Fig. 3: Verification latency as a function of message size for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-65, on a log-log scale across four message sizes spanning 100 bytes to 100 kilobytes.
Median of 10 Go testing.B runs per (key type, message size), each with testing.B's own iteration count, on an AMD Ryzen 5 7600X with 6 logical CPUs under Go 1.26.5; ML-DSA-65 is measured through the Cosmos SDK v0.55 key type the chain uses, which re-parses the packed key on every call.
ML-DSA-65 verification is 2.1 times faster than secp256k1 at 1 kilobyte (45 versus 95 microseconds), the reverse of signing, but its cost grows faster with message size and it is the slower of the two at 100 kilobytes (216 versus 136 microseconds); transactions sit at the small end of this range.
