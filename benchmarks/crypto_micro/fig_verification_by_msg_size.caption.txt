Fig. 3: Verification latency as a function of message size for secp256k1 and ML-DSA-44, on a log-log scale across four message sizes spanning 100 bytes to 100 kilobytes.
Single Go testing.B run per (scheme, message size) on AMD Ryzen 5 7600X under Go 1.23.5; the reported value is the mean over the framework's auto-tuned internal iteration count and run-to-run variance is not measured.
ML-DSA-44 verification is 3.3 times faster than secp256k1 at 1 kilobyte (40 versus 132 microseconds), the inverse of the signing relationship, because lattice verification reduces to a fixed handful of polynomial multiplications while ECDSA verification requires two modular exponentiations.
