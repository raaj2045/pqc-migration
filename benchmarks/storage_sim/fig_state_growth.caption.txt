Fig. 7: Cumulative on-chain account-state size as a function of Ethereum-to-Cosmos migrations for secp256k1 versus ML-DSA-65, on a log-log scale spanning 1 to 10 million migrations; solid lines show state at migration, dashed lines state after every migrated account has signed once.
Deterministic computation by tools/storage_sim/main.go: every transaction is one relayer-signed MsgRecvPacket of 4,513 bytes (measured median), and each migration creates one new receiver account that stores no public key until its owner first signs; no randomness or measurement error.
At 10 million migrations account state is 953.7 mebibytes under either scheme (1.00x), because the receiver's key type is not on chain at migration; if every migrated account then signs once, ML-DSA-65 reaches 19.11 gibibytes versus 1.24 gibibytes for secp256k1, a 15.43x ratio set by the public key (1,952 bytes versus 33 bytes).
