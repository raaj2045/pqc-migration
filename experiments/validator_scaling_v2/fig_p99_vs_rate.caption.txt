Fig. 10: Ninety-ninth-percentile transaction inclusion latency as a function of submitted load for secp256k1 versus ML-DSA-44, three subplots one per validator-set size N in {4, 7, 16}, with a logarithmic ordinate and a light gray reference line at the 10-second strict-criteria threshold.
Single 300-second run per (N, target rate, scheme) cell on AMD Ryzen 5 7600X under Go 1.23.5, four to sixteen Cosmos SDK validators in Docker containers each capped at one CPU; per-transaction commit time is the wall-clock moment our /block poller first observes the finalising block.
At target rates 10 and 50 both schemes complete inclusion in roughly six seconds (one block interval); the 10-second threshold is first crossed at rate 100 for every N and the gap between the two schemes never exceeds a factor of 1.3 in the saturated regime.
